Monthly operations meeting, January 2026

Present were Ada, Emre, Lena and Omar. The meeting opened with the monthly figures and the open actions from the last meeting. Customer satisfaction scores were read out region by region, and the support lead summarised the most common ticket types, which were delivery questions, invoice corrections and a smaller number of app problems. Stock levels for desks, chairs, lamps and shelves were checked against the forecast, and no product was below its safety stock. The finance update covered open invoices, supplier payments due in the coming weeks and the cash position, none of which needed a decision. Under any other business, the team reviewed the supplier scorecards, the warehouse safety checklist and the rota for the coming weeks. Each supplier was scored on delivery punctuality, damage rate and invoice accuracy, and the scores were filed with the purchasing records without a change of supplier. The safety checklist was signed off by the warehouse lead, and the rota was agreed as circulated. Revenue for the month was EUR 310,000. Late deliveries were 14% of all orders.

Emre was asked to talk to a second carrier for the West region and report back by 30 January. A trial budget of EUR 18,000 was approved by Ada.

The next meeting is planned for the second Monday of February.
